What are 2 player games?

Two player games are local multiplayer games designed for two people to play together on one screen. Both players share the same device and use a keyboard or touch controls to compete, cooperate, and take on the same challenges.

Many games split keyboard controls between WASD and the arrow keys, allowing both players to move at the same time. Competitive games focus on beating your opponent, while co-op games reward teamwork and communication.

Popular types of 2 player games

Action and fighting

Action and fighting games are all about timing, movement, and quick decisions. Face a friend in a one-on-one match or join forces against waves of enemies.

Try: Temple of Boom, Ragdoll Hit, Rooftop Snipers 2.

Sports

Sports games bring football, basketball, tennis, hockey, and more to the same screen. Aim, pass, shoot, and defend while sharing the keyboard.

Try: Basketball Stars, Football Legends, Tennis Masters.

Arcade and retro

Arcade games keep the rules simple and the action quick. Dodge hazards, jump between platforms, and compete for the best score.

Try: Poor Bunny, Tube Jumpers, Rowdy Wrestling.

Classics

Classic board and strategy games take turns instead of testing reflexes. Plan ahead, watch your opponent's moves, and aim for checkmate or a winning line.

Try: Master Chess, Four in a Row, Snakes and Ladders.

Racing

Two-player racing games put drivers on the same track and challenge them to reach the finish first. Some focus on precision, while others are all about chaotic collisions.

Try: Go Kart Go! Ultra!, Crazy Cars, Cyber Cars Punk Racing.

Co-op games

Co-op games ask both players to work toward a shared goal, from collecting items to solving platform puzzles and escaping tricky levels.

Platform games

Platform games mix jumping, timing, and obstacle courses. Co-ordinate jumps and find a safe route through each level.

Skill games

Skill-based games reward accuracy, reflexes, and careful timing. Quick rounds make them easy to pick up and replay.

Adventure games

Adventure games take two players through exploration, puzzles, and challenges that are easier with a partner.

Puzzle games

Puzzle games test planning and logic. Work together or challenge a friend to solve the board first.

How do you win a two-player game?

  • Points: Score more points than your opponent before the round ends.
  • Health: Avoid attacks and reduce your rival's health to zero.
  • Finish line: Reach the goal before the other player.

Frequently asked questions

What are 2 player games?

They are games made for two people to play together on one device, either in competition or in co-op mode.

Can two players use one keyboard?

Yes. Many local multiplayer games assign WASD to one player and the arrow keys to the other.

Are two-player games free to play?

The PlayTogether mini-game demos on this site are free to play in your browser.

Can we play competitively or as a team?

Both. Choose head-to-head games to compete or co-op games to work toward a shared objective.
